# Apple Crisp

## Ingredients
* 5 Honeycrisp, Granny Smith, or Cripp\s Pink apples, peeled, cored, sliced to 1/4" to 1/2" thick
* 2 tbsp cornstarch
* 2 tbsp fresh limon juice
* 2 tbsp granulated sugar
* 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
* 2 tsp ground cinnamon, divided
* 1 1/2 cups rolled oats
* 3/4 cup packed light brown sugar
* 2/3 cup all-purpose flour
* 1/4 tsp salt
* 3/4 cup unsalted butter, melted

## Instructions

1. Preheat oven to 350F
2. In a medium bowl, toss apples, cornstarch, lemon juice, granulated sugar, nutmeg, and 1 tsp cinnamon
3. In a small bowl, toss oats, brown sugar, flour, salt, and remaining 1 tsp cinnamon. Add butter and stir until a crumbly mixture forms resembling wet sand
4. Arrange apple mixture in a 2-quart ceramic baking dish. Top with oat mixture
5. Bake until apples are soft, topping is crisp and golden brown, and juices are bubbling around edges of dish, 55-70 minutes
