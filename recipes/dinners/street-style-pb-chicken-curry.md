# Street-Style Peanut Butter Chicken Curry
Bold, creamy, and irresistibly rich! This Street-Style Peanut Butter Chicken Curry brings together spicy street-food vibes with silky peanut sauce for a comforting, flavor-packed dish you'll crave again and again

## Ingredients
* 2 chicken breasts, cut into bite-sized pieces
* 1 onion, finely chopped
* 3 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 tbsp grated ginger
* 1 tbsp oil (vegetable or coconut oil)
* 1 red bell pepper, sliced
* 1 cup coconut milk
* 2 tbsp soy sauce
* 1 tbsp lime juice
* 1 tbsp brown sugar or honey
* 2 tbsp curry powder
* 1/2 tsp chili flakes
* 1/2 cup peanut butter
* 1/2 cup chicken broth or water
* Fresh cilantro for garnish
* Cooked rice for serving

## Instructions
1. Heat oil in a large pan and sauté onion until soft and golden. (Cooking Time: 4--5 minutes)
2. Add garlic and ginger, cooking until fragrant. (Cooking Time: 1--2 minutes)
3. Add chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned. (Cooking Time: 6--8 minutes)
4. Stir in curry powder and chili flakes to coat the chicken evenly. (Cooking Time: 1 minute)
5. Add coconut milk, peanut butter, soy sauce, chicken broth, and brown sugar, then mix well until smooth and creamy. (Prep Time: 3 minutes)
6. Simmer gently until the sauce thickens and chicken is fully cooked. (Simmer Time: 10--12 minutes)
7. Add red bell pepper and cook until slightly tender but still crisp. (Cooking Time: 3--4 minutes)
8. Finish with lime juice and adjust seasoning to taste. (Finishing Time: 2 minutes)
9. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot over rice. (Serving Time: 2 minutes)

Prep Time: 10 minutes | Cooking Time: 25 minutes | Total Time: 35 minutes
