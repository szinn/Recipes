# Air Fryer Korean Fried Chicken

## Ingredients
* 700 g chicken wings or boneless chicken pieces
* 2 tablespoons cornstarch
* 1 teaspoon garlic powder
* 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
* 1/2 teaspoon salt
* 1 tablespoon vegetable oil

Korean Sauce:
* 3 tablespoons gochujang
* 2 tablespoons soy sauce
* 2 tablespoons honey
* 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
* 1 tablespoon ketchup
* 2 cloves garlic, minced
* 1 teaspoon sesame oil 

## Instructions
* In a large bowl, toss the chicken with cornstarch, garlic powder, salt, pepper, and vegetable oil until well coated. ⏱️ 3 minutes
* Preheat the air fryer to 400°F (200°C). Arrange the chicken in a single layer. ⏱️ 5 minutes
* Air fry the chicken for 18–20 minutes, flipping halfway, until golden and crispy. ⏱️ 20 minutes
* While the chicken cooks, combine all sauce ingredients in a saucepan and simmer until slightly thickened. ⏱️ 5 minutes
* Toss the hot crispy chicken in the Korean sauce until fully coated. ⏱️ 2 minutes

Serve immediately, garnished with sesame seeds or green onions if desired.
