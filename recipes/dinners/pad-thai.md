# Pad Thai

## Ingredients

* 2 tbsp tamari
* 2 tbsp lime juice
* 2 tbsp packed brown sugar
* 2 tbsp peanut butter
* 2 tbsp rice vinegar
* 2 tsp chili paste
* pad thai noodles
* olive oil
* 2 tsp ginger
* 1 boneless chicken breast or tofu
* 1 egg
* cilantro + green onions
* 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
* 1 lime quartered

## Instructions

1. Combine siy sauce, brown sugar, peanut butter, rice vinegar, chili paste for the sauce
2. Boil water for noodles and cook according to instructions
3. Heat oil and saute ginger and garlic until fragrant
4. Add chicken/tofu and cook then remove and set aside
5. Add a bit of oil and cook egg stirring well
6. Add noodles and sauce and chicken/tofu and cook until heated through
7. Remove from heat, stir in bean sprouts
8. Serve and top with cilantro, peanuts, and squeezed lime
