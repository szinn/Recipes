# 4-Ingredient Single Serve Protein Brownie

## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup cottage cheese
* 2 tbsp cocoa powder
* 1 tbsp monk fruit sweetener or maple syrop
* 1 egg
* 1/2 tbsp chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions

* Add cottage cheese, cocoa powder, monk fruit sweetener, and egg to a blender. Blend until completely smooth.
* Pour into an 8oz ramekin or similar sized bowl. Top with chocolate chips if using.
* Microwave for 1 minute then continue in 30 second increments until the center is cooked to your liking, about 2-2.5 minutes total.
* Let cool for a few minutes and enjoy!
